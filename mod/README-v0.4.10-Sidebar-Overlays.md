# v0.4.10 — Compact 3D planner sidebar and docked overlays

- Six small vertical sidebar controls replace the large bottom button bar.
- Economy and Districts open in a right-hand dock inside the same live 3D planner, preserving camera position, zoom and orientation.
- Economy: live Crown/iron/wheat, population, jobs, taxes, income and wages, with server-authorized deposit/tax actions.
- Districts: select, scroll, develop, delete (requires confirmation) and edit territory with the existing on-terrain chunk brush.
- Zone land and Architect also use the dock; Architect first-person actual-block hologram flow is retained.
- Press Esc to close a dock first; press Esc again to leave the 3D planner.
- 3D controls remain WASD, Q/E, right-drag, middle-drag, mouse wheel and Shift.

All economy and district actions are still validated by the server. No existing world save migrations are required.

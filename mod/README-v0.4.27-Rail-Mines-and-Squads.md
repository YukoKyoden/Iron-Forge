# v0.4.27 — Rail mines and squad commands

## Mine setup
Use the Architect Wand, choose **Select Mine Entrance rail**, and click the first rail at the opening. Scan, then confirm. A Mine Marker appears above the entrance column, above the surface and outside the tunnel's three-block clearance. Keep open sky above it; replace a missing marker at the same saved position to resume. Mine Markers drop themselves, can be crafted from one rail and four cobblestone, and appear in the creative tab.

Connected rails must have three blocks of width and three blocks of clear height, including the rail level. Curves need clearance in both directions. Slopes use actual reciprocal rail endpoints. Only sections at Y=32 or lower generate iron. Every valid section supplies 0.5 iron per six-minute cycle; half an ingot persists between cycles and world reloads. No coal or stone is generated by registered rail mines. Ordinary mining is unchanged.

Only loaded rails and tunnel space in the same kingdom's claims count. A break or blocked section removes downstream output. Rails above Y=32 can connect the surface to the productive tunnel. Surveys stop at 512 rails or 128 horizontal blocks from the entrance; maximum 32 registered mines. Loops count once. Track cannot be registered twice; connecting two registered mines pauses the later mine. Remove obsolete registrations through the kingdom building manager. Old box-selected mines must be unregistered and registered again by selecting a rail.

## Squad controls
Existing army names, reserves, casualties and positions remain saved. Create a named squad on the army map. **Recruit +10/+50** spends shared stockpile iron/wheat directly into the selected squad; a working registered barracks must be within 96 blocks, the squad within 20 blocks of the control point, and total troop capacity must permit recruitment. Recruitment costs remain three iron and eighteen wheat per troop. Existing reserves can still be assigned.

Left-click to select. Right-click to move; right-click enemy claimed terrain for an attack order against enemy soldiers. Right-drag or middle-drag pans without issuing orders. Hold, Follow, and Retreat are the first visible orders. Escape clears selection before closing. Map positions refresh every three seconds. Formation and uniform controls remain.

Nearby ordered squads automatically field at most 30 soldier entities per kingdom within the local area. Each soldier represents up to ten troops, with proportional health and attack. Larger squads keep remaining strength in their ledger and feed replacements into free local slots. Distant squads travel numerically at the existing 1.5 blocks/second. Loaded soldiers outside every player's observation range return to numerical strength only when all living squad entities are accounted for and combat has ended. Unloaded/missing entities never create replacement troops. Physical squads determine their map position from their soldiers and cannot also advance numerically. Follow pauses on commander logout/dimension change. Retreat ignores combat. Gather soldiers preserves survivors in the squad ledger; missing or fighting units block gathering. Operation commitments retain their existing rules.
